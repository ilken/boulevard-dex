# Brand Voice — Boulevard Dex

---

## Creative Analogy

**This product should feel like:**
A city boulevard at 1 a.m.: wet asphalt reflecting deep navy, one amber neon script humming over the storefront, pink taillight streaks and cyan streetlight pools — and a premium diecast parked under the light, Real Riders gleaming.

## Typography Rules

**Display font** (headings, stats, dex numbers): Montserrat 700/800.

**Body font** (paragraphs, UI text): Montserrat 400/500.

**Wordmark only**: Yellowtail (retro neon script) — used exclusively for the "Boulevard Dex" logo. Never for headings, body, or UI text.

**Explicitly banned fonts**: Inter, Roboto, Arial, Helvetica, Lato, Open Sans, Source Sans Pro.

**Why banned**: These fonts signal "I let an AI pick" or "default Bootstrap". The product deserves a distinct voice.

**Size scale**: Use the design token scale defined in `DESIGN-TOKENS.md`. Never hardcode `px` sizes.

---

## Tone of Voice (Copy)

**One-word summary**: Confident.

**Write like**: A collector talking to another collector — knows the line cold, celebrates it without gushing. "150 and counting." "Metal on metal. Real Riders. Walmart shelves only."

**Never write like**: Marketing copy. Exclamation points. Vague superlatives. Emoji.

**UI microcopy examples**:
- Empty state: "No cars match. Loosen the filters."
- Error/corrupt data: "Couldn't read your garage. Starting fresh."
- CTA: "Track your collection" not "Start your collecting journey today!"
- The line's hook, used in the hero: Boulevard is the only Hot Wheels premium line with continuous numbering across years — one dex, 2020 to now.

---

## Visual Don'ts

- No AI purple/pink gradients as base surfaces (pink is a neon *accent*, never a wash)
- No emoji as icons
- No light mode — this is a night street, permanently
- Neon glow is earned: hover states and brand moments only, never on every element
- No stock photography backdrops; asphalt navy surfaces carry the scene
